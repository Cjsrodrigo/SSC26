# Privacy Policy for Expressum

**Last updated:** 2026-03-29

Expressum respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect
Expressum does not collect, store, or share personal data.

## How the App Works
Expressum is an offline Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) app. All core features work locally on the device.

## Data Storage
Any preferences or app settings are stored locally on the user's device and are not sent to external servers.

## Third-Party Services
Expressum does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or external tracking services.

## Children's Privacy
Expressum is designed to be accessible and simple to use. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

## Data Sharing
Expressum does not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties.

## Security
Because Expressum works offline and does not transmit personal information, user privacy risks are minimized.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any future changes will be reflected on this page.

## Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the support information provided on the app's App Store page.
